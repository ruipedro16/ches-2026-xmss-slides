\begin{frame}{TreeHash}

      \begin{itemize}
          \setlength{\itemsep}{4mm}
        \item Proving functional correctness of TreeHash was the most challenging part of the proof
        \item Computes the root of a subtree from its leaves: 
        \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Security Specification}: Expressed as a
          recursive function over an inductive data type for trees.
        \item \textbf{RFC Specification}: Iterative function that
          makes use of a stack for storing intermediate nodes. 
        \end{itemize}
        \item Rather than proving that implementation and specification are
accessing the stack within bounds, we prove
that they perform identical accesses (which could be within bounds or out of bounds), and
prove that they produce the same result.
      \end{itemize}

\end{frame}
